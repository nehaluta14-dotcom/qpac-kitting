Material Readiness Process

KITTING

Warehouse picks required material by source.

→
WAREHOUSE QC

Warehouse checks kit accuracy before delivery.

→
T-3 DELIVERY

Kit delivered to production staging/location by 6 PM.

→
T-2 PC VERIFICATION

Production Control physically verifies the kit.

→
T-2 SHORTAGES

Missing parts entered into Central Shortage Report.

→
T-1 RECOVERY

Warehouse / owner recovers remaining material.

→
T0 READY

Complete verified kit available for production.

Example: Production starts 10/10/2026 at 6:00 AM → T-3 Delivery 10/07 at 6 PM → T-2 Verification 10/08 at 6 PM → T-1 Recovery 10/09 at 6 PM.
