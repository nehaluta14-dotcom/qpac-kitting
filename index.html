Proposed SYSPRO-Connected Material Flow

SYSPRO Pick Ticket

Stock code, description, required quantity, warehouse location and barcode are loaded.

→
Warehouse Picking

Picker uses the existing Right Scan scanner. Right Scan posts the transaction to SYSPRO; this portal reads the posted kitted quantity.

→
Part Label

Completed line generates a traceable label for the physical material.

→
Warehouse QC

QC scans/verifies physical material against the pick ticket.

→
T-3 Delivery

Verified material is delivered to the production staging area.

→
T-2 Production Control

Production Control scans the same material labels and confirms quantity received.

→
T-1 Recovery

Missing material is recovered and closed after Production Control confirms receipt.

For this demonstration, SYSPRO reads and transactions are simulated locally. The UI deliberately separates the ERP adapter so IT can later replace the mock functions with the company's approved SYSPRO 8 integration.
