T-3 → T-2 → T-1 → T0 Material Readiness Process

T-3 • Kit Delivery

Warehouse completes and delivers the kit by 6:00 PM.

Kit becomes available for physical verification.

→

T-2 • Verification

Production Control physically verifies the delivered kit.

Each missing part is entered into the Central Shortage Report.

→

T-1 • Recovery

Warehouse / Material Owner recovers missing material.

Recovered quantities are updated until all shortages are closed.

→

T0 • Production

Production begins with a complete verified kit.

Goal: no operator leaves the floor searching for material.

Example: Production starts 10/10/2026 at 6:00 AM

T-3 → 10/07/2026 6 PM – Kit Delivery
T-2 → 10/08/2026 6 PM – Verification
T-1 → 10/09/2026 6 PM – Shortage Recovery
T0 → 10/10/2026 6 AM – Production
