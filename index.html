Material Control Workflow

1. Warehouse Picker

Pick ERP pick ticket by source. Record stock shortages and submit completed pick.

→
2. Warehouse QC

Independently verify picker quantity. Record discrepancies and submit QC.

→
3. T-3 Delivery

Deliver verified material to production staging and confirm delivery.

→
4. T-2 Production Control

Verify received material by part and quantity. Submit discrepancies.

→
5. T-1 Recovery

Warehouse recovers shortages. Production Control confirms receipt and closes each shortage.

Control principle: Warehouse records what it picked, Warehouse QC independently verifies it, and Production Control performs the final receiving verification. A shortage remains visible until recovery is physically received and confirmed.
