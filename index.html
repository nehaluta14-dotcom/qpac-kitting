T-3 → T-2 → T-1 → T0 Material Readiness Process

T-3 • Kit Delivery

Warehouse completes and delivers the kit by 6:00 PM.

Kit becomes available for physical verification.

→

T-2 • Verification

Production Control verifies required material.

Each missing part is entered into the Central Shortage Report.

→

T-1 • Recovery

Warehouse / Owner recovers missing parts and updates recovered quantity.

Target: all shortages closed by 6:00 PM.

→

T0 • Production

Production begins with a complete verified kit.

Goal: no operator leaves the floor to search for material.

Example: Production 10/10/2026 6:00 AM → T-3 delivery 10/07 6 PM → T-2 verification 10/08 6 PM → T-1 recovery 10/09 6 PM → T0 production.
